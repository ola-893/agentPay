# Story choices

1. **Follow one funded session — selected.** The actual session travels through agent,
wallet, escrow and service, then into source code and saved evidence. It explains both
reuse and the need to stop. A progressively assembled diagram hands off to the recorded
transaction split. Restrained local narration, quiet instrumental bed, sparse cut sounds.
2. The shutdown first: open with cancellation and trace backward to the observation that
caused it. Strong incident form, but less direct for first-time judges.
3. A wallet's decision desk: requests arrive as cases and the camera tours each check.
Bold mechanical analogy, but risks obscuring the real implementation.

The user supplied the storyboard and requested the finished file, so option 1 follows it
without an extra selection stop. Opening: many request marks meet a single funded session.

Revision: the user selected a 90–120 second strengths-led cut. The session remains
the story carrier; limitations and future-work beats are removed, and the close is
“Fund once. Keep working.” Proof beats replace the unavailable dashboard chapter.
